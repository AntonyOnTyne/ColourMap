HOW TO PLAY

ColourMap instructions

In both games, watch the colour sequence, then rebuild it from memory using the palette.

Memory Expert

  • Play ten rounds. The sequence grows by one tile each round, from one tile to ten.
  • You have five seconds to memorise the colours and their order in every round.
  • Choose the matching colours from the palette in the same order.

Classic Timer

  • Play ten rounds, with six tiles in every sequence.
  • Memorisation time decreases by one second each round, from ten seconds in round one to one second in round ten.
  • Choose all six matching colours from the palette in the same order.

For both games

  • When memorisation ends, the sequence is hidden. Fill it from left to right by selecting palette colours.
  • Every choice is final. A wrong colour ends the game immediately.
  • Your thinking time adds up across rounds. It pauses during memorisation and between rounds.
  • Complete all ten rounds to win.

Daily attempts and stats

  • You can start up to ten games per mode each local calendar day.
  • Your attempts, scores, streaks, and stats are saved on this device and do not sync to other devices.
